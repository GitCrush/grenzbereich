Landscape works best. Left half: swipe up for throttle, down for brake – the further from where your thumb landed, the more pedal. Right half: slide your thumb left and right on the bar at the bottom to steer, like the mouse; it stays where you leave it. Handbrake bottom left, gears and view top right.
